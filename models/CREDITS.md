# Camellia model

Base asset: **Cat** by **J-Toastie**, published October 29, 2023.
Source: https://poly.pizza/m/DJ9rpAhrh3
Asset URL: https://static.poly.pizza/8b3fc8b2-7eae-4b19-8084-d2fbd725bc58.glb
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The GLB is redistributed unmodified. Runtime adaptations for this website include
blue/gold/cream coat colouring, skinned short-fur shells, smoothed normals,
nonmetallic materials, softened hindquarters, rounder head proportions,
added eyes and tapered, spring-animated whiskers, shortened muzzle and broader cheeks,
procedural joint poses, two-bone leg IK,
and animation/interaction control for Camellia.
The original asset includes an idle animation; walk/run/care motions are authored
in this project, not motion-capture data or original supplied walk clips.
Visible attribution is in Camellia's profile card.
